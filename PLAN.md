to Sunday, and W0 starts Monday 26 Oct. W0–W3 were the Galaxy Run block, raced in 2:08:06 on
22 Nov. Its plan and review are in `LOG.md` (29 Oct to 22 Nov).

changes when the data says it should, and every change goes in `LOG.md`.

**Gym**, three sessions from 30 Nov. Until then, only Upper A on Mondays, light. Two goals:
- strength and durability for the race
- physique: chest, shoulders, back, arms, abs and legs

How the week is split:
- **Upper / Legs / Upper.** The upper body gets two sessions, so every physique muscle is hit
  twice a week.
- **The legs get one heavy session.** Every run and ride trains them too, and leg soreness is
  what costs the key sessions. Rønnestad & Mujika use two heavy leg sessions a week in
  preparation phases; one is the price of the physique work. If the squat and the RDL stall by
  the W12 review, a second, short leg session goes in.
- **Durability lives in Legs:**
  - single-leg work
  - slow calf raises, for the cramps
  - the Copenhagen plank
  - band walks for the hips
- **Shoulders for the swim:**
  - band rotator-cuff work in every upper warm-up, and face pulls
  - no dips
  - no heavy barbell overhead press

**How hard:**
- **Legs:** always leave 2–3 reps in reserve. Keep the loads moderate in W5–W7, so Thursday's
  run is not sore.
- **Upper body:** leave 1–2 reps in reserve on the last set, or 2–3 in the first three weeks.
- **Tempo:** lower every rep over 2–3 s, the calf raises above all.
- **Progression:** when every set reaches the top of its rep range, add the smallest step the
  next time.
- **Technique:** if the gym offers an induction, use it in W4 to learn the squat, the RDL and
  the bench.
- **Logging:** record every session on the watch as Strength, so the sets and reps reach the
  table.
- **Order:** straight sets, warm-up included. Finish every set of one exercise before you start
  the next, so each station is set up once. No supersets and no alternating pairs.
- **Rest:** the rest times are guides. On the watch, rest steps end when you press lap, so you can
  share a machine or work in with someone.

- **The test:** Zwift's FTP Test [Standard], paced as in **Tests**: start the 20 min at about
  140 W. No Tab during the workout: it skips the block.

**W4 review, 29 Nov:** read the FTP test and the swim benchmark, then rebuild the Base weeks for
sub-6:30.

## Tests

**FTP** on Sun 29 Nov, Tue 19 Jan and Tue 9 Mar: Zwift's **FTP Test [Standard]** on the trainer,
set up as in **The trainer** under The week. The same test every time, so the three compare.
- About 45 min. The warm-up ends with 3 min at 110 % and 2 min at 120 % of Zwift's FTP, then
  20 min of free ride: no target, the gears set the power, and ERG is off. FTP is 95 % of the
  20-min average. Each block is a lap in the file.
- **Before the test, set Zwift's FTP to the last tested FTP**, 140 W for the first, so the warm-up
  targets fit. If the 120 % block feels harder than 8/10, turn ERG off and finish it by feel: the
  warm-up isn't scored.
- **No Tab during the workout.** Inside a workout it skips the block.
- **Not Zwift's ramp tests:** one protocol keeps the three tests comparable, 20 minutes is closer
  to a 3-hour race ride, and the ramp's 75 % of the best minute assumes a typical rider.
- If a test has to happen outdoors and 20 uninterrupted minutes aren't possible, do 2×8 min with
  10 min easy between. FTP is then 90 % of the average of the two.
- **After every test, set FTP on the Edge 540, the watch, Garmin Connect and Zwift.** Until then
  the IF and TSS in the table are wrong.

**Pacing the 20 min.** Nobody knows a power is holdable before the test; the test finds out.
Start a little under, check at fixed points, and add only what the effort proves.
- **Start** at the last tested FTP, which is 5 % under its 20-min power. For the first test, about
  140 W: the best 5, 10 and 20 min on record (24 Nov) are 147, 132 and 126 W, none of them all-out.
- **No change before minute 5.** The start always feels easy, because HR lags 2–3 min.
- **Check at minutes 5, 10 and 15:** breathing deep and rhythmic, with a few words possible; HR in
  a slow creep; cadence steady in the same gear. All three hold: add 5 W.
- **Red flags:** breathing out of control before minute 10, HR still climbing several beats a
  minute after minute 8, or cadence sagging in the same gear. Drop 10 W and hold. Never stop.
- **The last 2–3 min:** everything left.
- **On screen:** hold the 3-second number in a narrow band, about 138–145 W at the start of the
  first test, and move the band by the checks.
- A slightly low FTP is cheap: the next test corrects it. A fade costs the whole test.

**Swim benchmark** on Sat 28 Nov:
- the longest continuous freestyle at an easy effort
- a 100 m freestyle time
- a 200 m breaststroke time
- Hand-time all three. The watch doesn't count freestyle: on 14 Nov it logged 2 freestyle
  lengths.

**CSS test** on Sat 23 Jan and Sat 13 Mar, once 400 m continuous is possible.
- 400 m time trial, 10 min easy, then a 200 m time trial.
- CSS per 100 m = (T400 − T200) / 2.

**Aerobic trend:** every review reads the pace at HR 150–155 over the first 30 min of the Saturday
long run, against `weather_feels_c`. It needs no extra session.

**Checkpoint at the W19 review (~14 Mar):** read the two split lines against FTP, CSS, continuous
swim and long-run HR. Then set race power and run caps.

## Guardrails

Read everything over 7-day windows.

- **HRV below the floor for 3+ days, or poor readiness:** if 7-day HRV is below the baseline floor
  (48 ms today) for 3+ days, or readiness is below 30 on 3 of the last 5 mornings, the next
  quality session goes easy and the gym drops to 2 sets.
- **Sustained strain:** if 7-day HRV is below the floor for 7+ days, or Garmin says STRAINED at
  two reviews in a row, start the recovery week now and shift the block by a week.
- **Short night:** under 5 h before an early start, do that session easy and shorter. Never pair
  two short nights with two quality sessions.
- **ACWR:** above 1.5, keep next week's hours flat.
- **Long runs:** none more than 10 % longer than the longest of the last 30 days.
- **Pain:** if it changes your stride or gets worse as you run, stop. Take 2 days without
  running, and see a physio if it lasts more than 3 days.
- **Zones:** at least 75 % of each week's run time in Z0–2. Long runs at HR ≤ 157 outside their
  planned segments.

## Fuel, heat, sleep, cramps

**Carbohydrate:**
- On the bike: 60 g an hour in Base 1, 75 g in Base 2, 90 g from Build. Use glucose and fructose
  products.
- Runs over 75 min: 30–60 g an hour.
- Race morning: breakfast 2.5–3 h before the start, about 100 g of carbohydrate from food you
  know, and a gel 10 min before the start. That was the Galaxy Run routine, rehearsed on 8 Nov.
  Use it on the 4 Apr simulation.
- Every long session is gut training for race day (Jeukendrup).

**Eating for the gym:**
- Protein about 1.6–2 g/kg a day, which is 100–125 g at 62 kg (Morton 2018). Spread it over 3–4
  meals, one of them soon after each gym session.
- No dieting in this block. How much the abs show depends mostly on body fat, but eating too
  little costs the training.
- Weigh in once a week, on the same morning, in Garmin Connect, so `weight_kg` fills in. A
  steady drop means eat more.

**Sweat rate:**
- Weigh yourself nude before and after three long sessions in different heat.
- Sweat rate in L/h = (kg lost + litres drunk) / hours.
- Drink about 60–80 % of that.
- Start at about 500 mg of sodium an hour. Move toward 1,000 mg if your kit dries white.

**Heat:**
- The 70.3 run will happen around 10:30–13:00 in full sun.
- The Saturday long runs from 06:30 and the Sunday brick runs at 08:00–09:00 are the heat
  practice.
- Rehearse the race cooling: ice in the cap and the suit, cold sponges, cold drinks.

**Cramps:** in 210 Ironman triathletes, cramps went with earlier cramping and with running faster
than usual. They did not go with dehydration or blood sodium (Schwellnus 2011).
- The first fix is pacing and durability at race intensity.
- Fuel and sodium come second.
- Log every cramp with the km, the heat, the sleep and what you took.

**Sleep:**
- Keep the lights-out times in the week table, and nap on weekend afternoons.
- In race weeks, spend 8 h in bed Monday to Friday. Friday night counts most: before the Galaxy
  Run it was 3.1 h.
- Reviews read sleep as a 7-day average. The 2021 consensus (Walsh) recommends an individual
  sleep plan rather than a fixed 7–9 h.

## Equipment and admin

- **Aero bars, then one bike fit.** The RS5's stock cockpit is a one-piece alloy aero bar and stem
  with no round section, so standard 31.8 mm clip-ons don't clamp to it.
  - **Target: on by Sun 20 Dec.** Race power comes from tested FTP, and power on the bars is
